## Method

- Every primary URL is on an official company domain and was opened during source review. Original engineering postmortems, official service-health incident reports, or official status records are used as the primary evidence.
- Record evidence notes identify the source facts reviewed; the report links to each primary page. Current Google Cloud pages include their later full incident analysis where available.
- ?Verified? means the primary source supports the incident identity/date and the stored technical, customer-impact, recovery, prevention, and metric claims. It is not a claim that IncidentIQ accessed internal monitoring or independently proved a company?s root-cause conclusion.
- The local validator checks schema, unique IDs/identity, HTTPS, official-company-domain allowlists, category taxonomy, and exact synchronization of the root JSON mirror. To run it: `python scripts/validate_real_incidents.py` from `backend`. Optional `--check-sources` attempts HTTP retrieval of every primary URL.

## Checks and caveats

- 34 records contain one or more numeric or timestamp values explicitly reported in the source; one record has no published metric captured.
- Derived duration fields are labeled as duration/window descriptions and backed by source timestamps where the source gives start/end times. Calculated values are not treated as additional company measurements.
- No revenue loss, customer count, SLA compensation, or failed-request percentage is inferred as an established fact. Where unavailable, the dataset says ?Not disclosed in the official incident report.?
- The AWS SimpleDB page identifies a June 13 event but does not state the incident year; it was excluded because the schema requires an incident date.
- Dropbox states the event occurred Friday evening and publishes its postmortem on Sunday, 12 January 2014. The stored 10 January date is calendar-derived from that weekday and publication date; it is explicitly identified as derived in the record data-gap note.
- Status pages can be revised over time. Google?s 2024 records were reviewed against the full reports currently present, not just their early mini-status updates.
- All verified records are stored as an immutable JSON catalog; no operational database was seeded or modified by data import.
